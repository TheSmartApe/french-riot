About

French Riot is a citizen information page announcing the next major demonstration in Paris: where it takes place, when it starts and how to get there.

On October 17, the people will gather on Place Charles de Gaulle, at the foot of the Arc de Triomphe, to make their voice heard. Peaceful, determined, impossible to ignore.

Everything lives on this single page. When a detail changes, it is updated here first — check back before you travel.
